# Enterprise Dashboard Design Standard

## Principles

1. **Information Hierarchy**:
   - Executive KPIs (Top Cards): Big numbers with clear context, deltas, and period comparisons.
   - Core Funnel / Conversions: Distinct company-level and contact-level funnels.
   - Activity & Leaderboards: Dense, scannable lists and charts.

2. **Restrained Data Visualization**:
   - Avoid visual clutter and neon rainbow charts.
   - Use curated brand tokens (`--color-primary`, `--color-accent`, muted neutrals).

3. **Responsive Density**:
   - Maintain high information density on desktop screens (1920x1080 down to 1280x800) without excessive whitespace.
